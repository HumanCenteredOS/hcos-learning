## Item Quality Review

Confirm:

- the stem presents a meaningful clinical decision;
- one answer is clearly preferred;
- distractors are plausible;
- distractors are incorrect or less appropriate for defensible reasons;
- the question does not depend on a trick;
- the question avoids unnecessary obscurity;
- the wording avoids double negatives;
- "all of the above" is not used;
- "none of the above" is not used;
- the item tests application, interpretation, or reasoning where feasible;
- the item aligns with the stated learning objective;
- the correct answer can be traced to the evidence needed to support it;
- the explanation does not overstate certainty.

---

## Evidence and Provenance Check

For the correct answer and each evidence-dependent distractor rationale, ask:

1. What exact proposition is being taught?
2. What source would support it?
3. Is it a guideline recommendation, study finding, consensus, interpretation, or inference?
4. Does the source support the complete proposition?
5. Is the explanation stronger than the evidence?
6. Is contradictory evidence relevant?
7. Does the item inadvertently teach an unsupported rule as universal?
