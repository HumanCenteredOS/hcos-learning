## Case Provenance Review

Before finalizing, ask:

1. Does each preferred approach have a defensible evidence basis?
2. Are recommendations attributed?
3. Are important alternatives represented fairly?
4. Does the case imply certainty beyond the evidence?
5. Does any decision depend on an unacknowledged inference?
6. Does a citation support the exact proposition to which it is attached?
7. Does evidence supporting one case element improperly support the entire narrative?
